👸🚀 Space Princess

🪐⭐🌙

For grown-ups 👋
A gentle learning game for ages 4–6. Each planet is a short challenge: counting, adding, numbers, more/less, letters, first sounds, 3-letter words and rhymes. Block Buddies planet: build numbers block by block, drag buddies together to add, tap to split (number bonds), "Make me a number", odd/even pairs, and number stairs. Every prompt is read aloud (tap 🔊 to repeat). There's no way to fail — wrong taps get a kind hint. Difficulty rises slowly after several correct answers in a row and eases off after mistakes. Progress is saved on this device only.
Reset clears stars, gems, stickers and days played. Settings stay.
