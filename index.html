🏡 Zippet's Walk Home

🌳🌼🏡


For grown-ups 👋

A gentle learning game for ages 4–6. Each game is a short challenge: counting, adding, numbers, more/fewer, letters, first sounds, 3-letter words, rhymes and Sound Blending (blend sounds into a word, and tap sound boxes to split a word into sounds). Letters and sounds come in school order (s a t p i n → m d g o → c k e u r → h b f l → j v w x y z q); the next group unlocks when she gets 8 of the last 10 right first time, or after 30 tries anyway. Quick Look: see small amounts at a glance, ten-frames, and pairs that make 5 and 10. Adding includes take away at higher levels. The game quietly tracks each letter, number, word and fact and brings weaker ones back now and then (at most 1 question in 4). A first-try answer earns a star ⭐; an answer after a hint earns a sparkle ✨ (two sparkles make a star). Zippet's Blocks: build, join, split, make, odd/even pairs and number stairs. Every prompt is read aloud (tap 🔊 to repeat). There's no way to fail — wrong taps get a kind hint. Progress is saved on this device only.
