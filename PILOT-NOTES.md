# How to run a 5-session pilot (for a grown-up)

This is a paper guide. The game tracks nothing for this, and nothing goes online. Use it to see what she finds easy or hard, so you can tell what to change.

## Set-up
- Do 5 short sessions over about 2 weeks, 10–15 minutes each, at a time she is fresh (not right before bed).
- Sit beside her, but let her lead. Don't help unless she asks or gets stuck for more than about 10 seconds.
- Print the table below, or copy it into a notebook.

## The 5 sessions
1. **Free play.** She picks anything. Note which places she picks first and which she avoids.
2. **Maths.** Counting, Quick Look, Adding, Tens and Ones and Zippet's Blocks.
3. **Letters and sounds.** Letters, First Sounds, Blending, Trace.
4. **Words and stories.** Words, Story Corner (try both "Read to me" and "I'll read"), Sight Word Stars.
5. **Today's Adventure.** Then open the Progress report together (grown-ups area) and compare it with your notes.

## What to write down
- **Item:** the exact letter, number, word or question.
- **What she did:** for example "picked 6 for 9", "said 'buh' for d" or "traced the 2 backwards".
- **Hint used?** Yes or no.
- **Mood:** 🙂 😐 🙁
- **Anything confusing in the game itself:** a picture, a voice clip, a button.

## Afterwards
- Look for items that went wrong 3 or more times. Those are the ones to practise offline. The report's "Things to try together" ideas can help.
- List any clip that sounded wrong (especially the letter sounds) or any picture she misread, and ask for those to be fixed.
